Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Setup

  • Measured items go under clear trade headings.
  • Every BOQ item gets a number, a unit and a short description.
  • You get an Excel file you can edit, price and keep using.

Rebar & Bar Bending Schedules (BBS)

A Bar Bending Schedule needs enough reinforcement detail on the structural drawings. When it's there, I list the bar marks, diameters, shapes, cutting lengths and total steel. I send it in Excel, so you can check it, plan fabrication or ask suppliers for prices.

5D BIM Quantity Takeoffs

If your IFC model is detailed enough, I take the volumes, areas and element counts out of it and put them in a CSV or Excel schedule. You review it from there.

Subcontractor Trade Takeoffs

Quantity schedules for one trade at a time, such as drywall partitions, certain MEP elements or reinforcement, so you can tender or price a subcontract.

Insurance Repair BOQs

Repair estimates broken down item by item, from whatever you can give me: site photos, damage reports, drawings or models. The more detail you send, the more complete the estimate gets.

How It Works

1Send Your Drawings

Email your architectural and structural drawings as PDFs, or an IFC model if you have a suitable one, to belayamba1984@gmail.com. Files too big for email? Upload them to Google Drive or Dropbox, click Share, set access to "Anyone with the link", then copy the link and email it to me. If the link isn't set to "Anyone with the link", I won't be able to open it. Tell me where the project is, what the scope is and any specifications I should know about.

2Review & Quote

I go through what you sent and look at the scope, how many sheets there are and how complicated the job is. Then I email you a price and a delivery time I can stick to. If anything's unclear, I'll ask you before I quote.

3Agree & Sign

When we agree on the price and delivery time, I email you the service agreement. You read it and we both sign electronically. I start as soon as the signed copy is back with me.

4Receive Deliverables

You get the deliverables we agreed on, in the formats written in the quotation. That's normally Excel and/or PDF. If revision rounds are included, the scope will say so.

Before we start: please send your company profile and a LinkedIn page for your business along with your drawings. I work with clients in other countries, so this is simply how I confirm I'm dealing with a real, registered contractor.

Payment stages:
